**Focus:** Deep-code audit (principal-engineer pass) and its remediation, then three rounds of operator testing against a REAL provider. The audit's headline finding was not bad design but disconnected design: row-level security was built, FORCEd and tested, yet the app connected as a superuser carrying BYPASSRLS, so every database-level guarantee was inert. Model tiering is designed but not wired, and picking a model name is not a guess to make on the owner's behalf. Also awaiting (a) R14 sign-off from Ayesha/Sami, (b) the consent / retention / relationship-based-access decisions (see `.ai/audit/REMEDIATION_BACKLOG.md`).
